Why space and chips — and why it matters to you

The mathematics that keeps a satellite on its orbit is the same that measures a distance on a map, and the atomic clocks of navigation satellites help keep everyday clocks on time. Space is where a calculation can be checked against independent programs and published references, so S.T.A.R. starts there and brings the same discipline to everyday checks. Research on chips asks how much computing a device can do for each watt — for phones, cars and data centres alike.

Check a whole table in one pass

Choose a CSV or text file, or paste a table, and tick the checks to run. Each check takes the columns of its kind; nothing is uploaded.

About this tool
What it solves
Check a register, an export or a spreadsheet in one go, instead of value by value, and keep a cleaned copy with a single receipt.
What it takes
A CSV or text file, or a pasted table. Example: a small register with tax codes, VAT numbers, IBANs, due dates and amounts, with some mistakes.
How to read the result
A summary for each check, the list of values read differently (before and after), the cleaned table to download and one receipt for all the checks.
Checks in the receipt
Each code goes through the same engines as the released checks, row by row; the tests compare every row with python-stdnum and read the cleaned table back to prove your columns are unchanged.
What it cannot guarantee
It marks values, it does not correct them; valid means formally valid; columns are chosen from the form of their values.
Checks to run

Distance between two places

Type two places as latitude and longitude (decimal, like 41.9028, or degrees-minutes-seconds, like 41°54'10"N), or pick a city.

About this tool
What it solves
The real distance and starting direction between two places on the Earth, not a straight line on a flat map.
What it takes
Two places as latitude and longitude (decimal or degrees-minutes-seconds), or picked from the list. Example: Rome to New York.
How to read the result
The big number is the distance along the Earth's surface (WGS-84 ellipsoid); directions are from North, clockwise; the sphere line shows how far off a simpler formula would be.
Checks in the receipt
The distance is walked back along the computed direction to see where it lands (round trip), and compared with a sphere; both are in the receipt.
What it cannot guarantee
Not a road, sea or flight route; no heights; not for navigation or safety decisions; only as accurate as the coordinates you type.

Convert coordinates

Type a latitude and a longitude in any common format.

About this tool
What it solves
Rewrite a position in the formats maps and GPS devices use, without copying mistakes.
What it takes
Latitude and longitude in decimal degrees or degrees-minutes-seconds, with N/S/E/W. Example: Milan cathedral.
How to read the result
Decimal degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds, and UTM (zone, easting and northing in metres).
Checks in the receipt
Each conversion is converted back and compared with your input; special and polar UTM zones are flagged.
What it cannot guarantee
No change of datum (everything is WGS-84); UTM is not defined near the poles.

Check a column of numbers

Choose a CSV file, or paste the text. From Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets: select the column with its title, copy, and paste it here (or save the sheet as CSV; .xlsx files cannot be opened directly). Separators , ; | and tab are recognised; Italian numbers like 1.234,5 are read correctly and every conversion is listed.

About this tool
What it solves
Find what is wrong in a column of numbers before you trust a total or an average.
What it takes
A CSV file or pasted text; separators , ; | or tab; Italian numbers such as 1.234,5; text saved by Excel on Windows. Example: daily readings with mistakes.
How to read the result
How many values, the mean and median, the spread, and the values to double-check; every converted, missing or unreadable cell is listed; you can download a checked copy.
Checks in the receipt
Mean and median are computed by released S.T.A.R. packages; suspicious values are flagged by a stated rule (modified z-score above 3.5).
What it cannot guarantee
It cannot determine whether a suspicious value is actually wrong; it identifies the values to review. It does not open .xlsx files.

Fit a straight line

Choose a CSV file with two columns of numbers, or paste them. The first column is x and the second y, unless you choose otherwise after the first run.

About this tool
What it solves
See whether one quantity grows with another, and by how much.
What it takes
Two columns of numbers (x and y) as a CSV file or pasted text. Example: energy used against hours of work.
How to read the result
The line y = slope × x + intercept, the uncertainty of each, R² and the typical distance of the points from the line; a plot.
Checks in the receipt
The fit is computed by a released S.T.A.R. package; the uncertainties come from how the points scatter.
What it cannot guarantee
A straight line only. It does not prove cause and effect, and its uncertainty assumes independent, evenly scattered points.

Check that a file is unchanged

Choose a file (up to 100 MB). Optionally paste the value it should have, as published by whoever sent it.

About this tool
What it solves
Show that a file is exactly the expected one — after a download, a copy, or before you send it.
What it takes
Any file, read only by your browser; optionally the value you expect (SHA-256, SHA-1, MD5 or CRC-32). Example: a short text file.
How to read the result
The fingerprints of the file; if you paste an expected value, a clear match or no match.
Checks in the receipt
SHA-256, SHA-1 and MD5 by Python's hashlib; CRC-32 by zlib and, for files up to 1 MB, also by the released star-crc, bit for bit.
What it cannot guarantee
A match proves the file is identical to the one the value was computed from — not that the file is safe, nor that the value came from someone you trust.

What it solves
Convert a value between units without slips of a power of ten.
What it takes
A number and two units of the same kind. Example: 26.2 miles in kilometres.
How to read the result
The converted value and the exact factor used.
Checks in the receipt
Factors from exact SI definitions in a released S.T.A.R. package; units of different kinds are refused.

Check an Italian tax code

Type the 16 characters (spaces and lower case are fine). Optionally add surname and name to check their letters.

About this tool
What it solves
Catch a mistyped tax code before it goes on an invoice, a contract or a form, and read what the code says.
What it takes
The 16 characters; optionally surname and name. Example: RSSMRA80A01H501U, the textbook example.
How to read the result
Valid or not; the sex and possible dates of birth coded in it; whether it is an omocode, with its original form.
Checks in the receipt
The check character recomputed (D.M. 23/12/1976 tables); omocodia letters in the official order; a real birth date; surname and name letters if given.
What it cannot guarantee
It cannot say the code was issued or belongs to a living person; the place of birth is shown as a code, not a name.

Check an Italian VAT number

Type the 11 digits; an IT prefix and spaces are removed and listed in the receipt.

About this tool
What it solves
Catch a mistyped VAT number before you issue or pay an invoice.
What it takes
11 digits, with or without IT. Example: a made-up number that only satisfies the rule (serial 0000001, office 001).
How to read the result
Valid or not; the serial and the office code; the check digit expected.
Checks in the receipt
The check digit with the Italian rule — which gives the same digit as the Luhn rule on 11 digits, and the receipt says so; the office code in its allowed ranges; a serial that is not zero.
What it cannot guarantee
It cannot say the number is active or whose it is: that is on VIES or at the Agenzia delle Entrate.

Check an IBAN

Type or paste the IBAN; spaces, lower case and a leading 'IBAN' are fine.

About this tool
What it solves
Catch a mistyped IBAN before a payment, and see how an Italian IBAN is made.
What it takes
An IBAN of any country that uses one. Example: IT60 X054 2811 1010 0000 0123 456, the example used in banking documents.
How to read the result
Valid or not; the IBAN in groups of four; for Italy the CIN, ABI, CAB and account.
Checks in the receipt
The length for the country (SWIFT registry); the two check digits by ISO 7064 MOD 97-10; for Italy the CIN letter.
What it cannot guarantee
A valid IBAN can still belong to someone else or to a closed account; the bank name is not looked up.

Working days and deadlines

Count the working days between two dates, or find the date N working days after a start. Dates as 2026-12-31 or 31/12/2026.

About this tool
What it solves
Count working days or set a deadline without forgetting Easter Monday or 4 October.
What it takes
Two dates, or a date and a number of working days; your patron saint's day if it is a holiday where you work. Example: December 2026.
How to read the result
How many working days, or the deadline and its weekday; which holidays fell in the period; possible bridge days.
Checks in the receipt
National holidays (L. 260/1949 as amended, 4 October from 2026); Easter by the Gregorian rule, checked to fall on a Sunday between 22 March and 25 April.
What it cannot guarantee
No regional, sector or company closures; not legal advice on how a legal deadline is counted (some rules move a deadline that falls on a holiday).

Compare two tables

Paste the old table in A and the new one in B, with a first row of column names (CSV, semicolons or tabs). The key is the column that identifies a row, such as a code; leave it empty to use the first column the two tables share.

About this tool
What it solves
See exactly what changed between two versions of a price list, an inventory or a register, without checking row by row.
What it takes
Two tables with column names and a key column. Example: a parts list before and after an update.
How to read the result
Rows removed and added; for each changed row, the column and the old and new value.
Checks in the receipt
Rows matched by key, duplicate keys refused; counts compared with pandas (merge and compare) on the same tables.
What it cannot guarantee
It matches rows only by the key; 0.10 and 0.1 are the same number, but 10 % and 0.1 are different values.

Find duplicate rows

Preview · not released yet Preview: this tool has passed its tests on a PC, and becomes a released product after its test on the published site. Until then no count on this site includes it.

Paste a table with a first row of column names. Choose how similar two rows must be to be reported (1 = identical apart from spaces, capitals, accents and punctuation, 0.9 = about one character in ten may differ), and optionally which columns to compare, separated by commas.

About this tool
What it solves
Clean a contact list or a register before you send, count or import it.
What it takes
A table with column names; how similar rows must be; the columns to compare. Example: five contacts, two of them written twice.
How to read the result
Groups of identical rows by line number, and pairs of nearly identical rows with their similarity.
Checks in the receipt
Spaces, capitals, accents and punctuation normalised before comparing; edit distance checked against rapidfuzz.
What it cannot guarantee
Similar is not the same person: two rows that look alike can be two people, and the same person written very differently is missed.

Check the VAT of an invoice

Preview · not released yet Preview: this tool has passed its tests on a PC, and becomes a released product after its test on the published site. Until then no count on this site includes it.

Paste the invoice lines: taxable amount, VAT rate in percent and, if you have it, the VAT written on the line. Columns are recognised by name (imponibile, aliquota, IVA; taxable, rate, VAT) or by position. Optionally type the total VAT on the invoice.

About this tool
What it solves
Catch a wrong VAT amount or a rounding difference before you issue, pay or record an invoice.
What it takes
The invoice lines (taxable, rate, VAT if written) and the total VAT if you want it checked. Example: five lines at 22, 10 and 4 %.
How to read the result
VAT line by line, totals by rate, the invoice total, and the gap between rounding each line and rounding the total.
Checks in the receipt
Decimal arithmetic to the cent, halves up; totals checked against exact fractions on the same lines.
What it cannot guarantee
It does not know special regimes (reverse charge, split payment, exemptions) or which rounding your system uses; not tax advice.

What a file says about you

Choose a photo (JPEG, PNG), a PDF or a Word, Excel or PowerPoint file. The file is read in your browser and never sent anywhere.

About this tool
What it solves
Before you send a photo or a document, see whether it carries your name, your device or the place where it was made.
What it takes
A JPEG, PNG, PDF, DOCX, XLSX or PPTX file. Example: a small photo with a camera name, an author and a position.
How to read the result
Every field found, what it reveals (person, device, place, dates, software, content), and a warning if there is a position.
Checks in the receipt
EXIF, XMP, PNG text, PDF information and Office properties read by the tool's own parser and compared with Pillow, exifread, pypdf, python-docx and openpyxl.
What it cannot guarantee
It shows the metadata but does not remove it; formats and fields it does not know are not read, so an empty report is not proof that the file is clean.

Why use this instead of a calculator website

  • Your data never leaves your computer.
  • The software is open source, released, and tested against independent programs — the tests are published.
  • Every step is listed in a receipt you can keep, print or attach to a report.
  • Nothing to install, no account, no adverts.

The 6 calculation tools run released S.T.A.R. packages. The 8 checks for documents, payments and dates run a small engine written for the Workspace (Python standard library only), and the one-pass table check applies the same engines to every row; each is released as a product after its own tests. More are coming; see the roadmap.

Real software benchmark Each tool's Python is tested on a PC against independent references: the 6 calculation tools, with the same published packages, against pyproj, numpy and zlib (71 tests); the 5 everyday checks against python-stdnum, schwifty, holidays, dateutil and numpy (83 tests for the 8 single checks, 37 for the one-pass table check, row by row). All pass at build time.
