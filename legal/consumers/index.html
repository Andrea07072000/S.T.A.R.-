1. To whom the service is addressed

The engineering assessment is designed for companies, research groups and professionals. Nothing on this website is sold to consumers. If a consumer (a person acting outside any business or profession) nevertheless signs a contract, every right the law gives consumers applies, whatever the contract says.

2. Right of withdrawal for consumers

A consumer who signs a contract at a distance may withdraw within 14 days without giving a reason (Codice del consumo, art. 52). If the consumer asks expressly for work to start before the 14 days end, and acknowledges that the right is lost once the service has been fully performed, the right ends when the work is complete (art. 59(1)(a)); if they withdraw before, they pay only for the part already performed.

3. Complaints

  1. Write to Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me with the subject "Complaint", saying what went wrong and what you ask.
  2. You receive an acknowledgement within 7 days and a reasoned answer within 30 days.
  3. If the answer does not satisfy you, mediation may be proposed before going to court; for consumers, the court of their place of residence is competent.

The European online dispute resolution platform (ODR) was closed on 20 July 2025 (Regulation (EU) 2024/3228) and is therefore not linked here.

4. Supervisory authorities

For personal data: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. For unfair commercial practices: Autorità Garante della Concorrenza e del Mercato.
