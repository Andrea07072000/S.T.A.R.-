1. Browsing and downloading: no minimum age

Anyone may read this website and download the software. The website collects no personal data from visitors (see the privacy notice), so browsing raises no question of consent.

2. Writing to the owner: 14 years

An email contains personal data. In Italy a person aged 14 or more may consent alone to the processing of their data by an information society service (Codice privacy, art. 2-quinquies). Under 14, please do not write without a parent or guardian. The website does not ask anyone's age and does not knowingly process data of children under 14: if the owner learns that such data were received, they are deleted, except what the law requires to keep.

3. Requesting a service and signing a contract: 18 years

The engineering assessment is offered only to adults with full legal capacity (Italian Civil Code, art. 2) acting for themselves or for an organisation they are entitled to bind. A request from a minor is not accepted. Before a contract, the owner may ask for the information needed to confirm who is signing.

4. Software for everyone

The software is free and published under the Apache License 2.0; the licence sets no age limit. It is engineering software: a minor who uses it for study does so under the responsibility of a parent or teacher.
