Not a space operator

S.T.A.R. writes and checks software for analysis on the ground. It does not launch, operate, command or control any space object, and its software cannot send commands to a spacecraft. It therefore does not carry out the space activities that require an authorisation under the Italian space economy law (Law 13 June 2025, no. 89). Anyone who uses S.T.A.R. software in a space mission remains responsible for the authorisations, insurance and decisions of that mission.

Export controls and sanctions

  • The public packages are general-purpose scientific software published openly; they implement textbook and published algorithms.
  • S.T.A.R. does not accept, in any request or project, data or technology subject to export restrictions (for example under EU Regulation 2021/821 on dual-use items, or the US EAR and ITAR) unless the required authorisations exist and have been agreed in writing.
  • No service is provided to persons or entities under EU or UN sanctions.
  • Users are responsible for complying with the export and sanctions rules that apply to them.
