2. What the website is

An information site about the S.T.A.R. software packages, the published verification evidence and the engineering assessment service that can be requested by email, with free tools that run in your browser (the Workspace). Nothing can be bought on the website and no account can be created.

The Workspace tools are free. What you type or open in them is processed on your device and is not sent to the owner; each result comes with a receipt that states the checks made and their limits.

Anyone may browse the website. Writing to the owner requires being 14 or older, and requesting a paid service or signing a contract requires being an adult (see minimum age). How paid work is agreed and invoiced is described in payments and invoicing.
