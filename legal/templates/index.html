Contract templates

Eight documents under Italian law — non-disclosure, engagement letter, software development, business software terms, online terms for consumers, continuing supply, data processing agreement, formal notice — each in every combination of its options, as a numbered PDF.

Templates to adapt, prepared without a lawyer's review: they are not legal advice and bind no one until signed. The Italian text is the binding one; the English is a courtesy translation. Have the final text checked by a professional.

In each PDF: a cover with the identifier and the options chosen; contents; numbered articles and clauses; signatures; the clauses that need a second signature under Articles 1341–1342 of the Civil Code, listed by their final number; the legal acts cited, with their official titles as verified; the SHA-256 of the text.

How every PDF is checked

  • Each PDF is read back with pypdf: every clause of the chosen options is present, and no clause of an option that is off.
  • No unfilled placeholder; "Page x of N" correct on every page; the printed SHA-256 equals the text's.
  • The specific-approval list matches the flagged clauses; accented letters survive; no two runs of text overlap.
  • The checker itself is tested on broken PDFs (wrong seal, missing clause, overlapping text): it must fail them.

Stress test on Google Cloud (8 vCPU): 3,840 PDFs — every variant with 40 fillings, including long names with accents, ampersands and markup-like text and descriptions of up to 400 words — 18,952 pages, 0 failures; rendering 73.9 s, checking 82.9 s.
