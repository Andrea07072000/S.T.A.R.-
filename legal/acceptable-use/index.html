1. What this policy covers, and what it does not

This policy applies to the use of this website and of the engineering assessment service. It does not restrict the software: the S.T.A.R. packages are licensed under the Apache License 2.0, which allows use for any purpose, and no rule on this page takes away a right that licence gives you. The law, of course, applies to everyone.

2. Not allowed on this website

  • Attempts to break into, overload or disrupt the website or the infrastructure behind it, including automated requests at a rate that degrades it for others. Reading and downloading, also by script, at a reasonable rate is welcome.
  • Using the website to distribute malware, or presenting copies of its pages or files as the original when they have been altered.
  • Using the S.T.A.R. name or logo to suggest that the owner endorses a product, a result or a person when he does not (see names and marks).

3. Not accepted in the assessment service

  • Work whose purpose is unlawful, or which would breach sanctions or export-control law (Regulation (EU) 2021/821 and the EU restrictive measures in force): see space activities and export control.
  • Material the requester has no right to share, or classified or export-controlled data sent without a prior written agreement and the authorisations required.
  • Requests to present an assessment as a certification, a qualification or an approval by an authority. An assessment reports measurements; it certifies nothing (see evidence and limits).

4. Security research

Good-faith research on the software is welcome and is governed by the security policy. Testing the website itself is limited to what does not degrade it for others.

5. What happens in case of breach

The owner may refuse or stop a request for service, and report unlawful conduct to the competent authorities. Nothing in this policy limits the rights that the law gives you.
