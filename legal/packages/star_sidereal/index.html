5. What it does, and its limits

GMST and local mean sidereal time, IAU 1982 (Vallado eq. 3-47); verified against Vallado and ERFA.

  • gmst82_deg(jd_ut1_day, jd_ut1_frac=0.0) -> degrees in [0, 360), Vallado eq. 3-47 (Aoki et al. 1982).
  • lst_deg(jd_day, jd_frac, east_longitude_deg) -> degrees in [0, 360).
  • Non-finite input raises ValueError. Input is UT1 (not UTC): the caller applies DUT1.
