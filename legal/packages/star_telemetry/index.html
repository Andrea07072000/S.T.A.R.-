5. What it does, and its limits

CCSDS AOS v2 / TM v1 transfer-frame deframer and Space Packet reassembler, with CUC time decoding via star-timescales.

  • CcsdsTransferFrameEngine(frame_length, has_fecf).parse_frame(bytes) -> (header, data_field): TFVN, SCID, VCID, VC frame count, replay flag, first header pointer, FECF (CRC-16, poly 0x1021, init 0xFFFF) validity.
  • process_raw_stream(bytes) reassembles Space Packets across frames per virtual channel, including spanning packets. A packet is delivered in the frame that completes it. After a lost frame, the rest of the interrupted packet is discarded up to the next first header pointer. A pending packet whose size disagrees with its declared length is dropped, never cut to size.
  • A frame of the wrong length is rejected with ValueError; a corrupted frame is reported (fecf_valid = False), never silently accepted.
