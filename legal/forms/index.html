Personal data request (GDPR)

Ask for access to, correction or erasure of your personal data, or exercise another right under Articles 15–22 GDPR.

 
Send it, if you wish, to Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me
Vulnerability report
Report a security problem in a S.T.A.R. package, following the security policy.
 
 
 
 
Send it, if you wish, to Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me
Assessment request
Describe the engineering question you want measured. A first message carries no confidential data; confidentiality is agreed before any is exchanged.
 
 
 
 
Send it, if you wish, to Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me
Mutual non-disclosure agreement (template)
Signed before confidential material of a prospective customer is received. A template: both parties read, adapt and sign it.
 
 
Send it, if you wish, to Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me
End-use statement
Asked before an assessment whose result could support a space or dual-use activity. It records who will use the result, where and for what.
 
 
 
 
 
 
Send it, if you wish, to Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me
Billing details
Sent once a contract is agreed, so that the receipt or the electronic invoice carries the right data.
 
 
 
 
Send it, if you wish, to Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me
Acceptance of an offer
Confirms in writing the acceptance of a numbered offer. It completes, and does not replace, the signed contract.
 
 
 
 
 
Send it, if you wish, to Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me
