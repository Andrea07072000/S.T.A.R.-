From research to applied software

Six case studies in which a research question produced dependable software, or established where reliance is not justified. Each states its baseline, data, hardware, cost and limitations.

Post-quantum key exchange from the standard alone

The key-exchange mechanism intended to protect messages, backups and connections against future quantum computers.

QuestionCan ML-KEM be implemented from FIPS 203 alone and reproduce the official test vectors?
BaselineNIST ACVP vectors (the official answers) and OpenSSL 4.0.1 / pyca cryptography 48.0.1.
DataNIST ACVP-Server ML-KEM keyGen and encapDecap JSON files, downloaded 2026-10-17, fingerprints recorded.
Result240 of 240 vectors byte-exact; 500 + 500 ML-KEM-768 and 500 + 500 ML-KEM-1024 exchanges interoperable with OpenSSL in both directions.
Hardware and costIntel Core i5-7200U laptop, 2 cores; free tools; no cloud.
LimitsPure Python: slow and not hardened against timing side channels. Not suitable for production secrets. Internal prototype, not yet a public package.

Collision probability compared with NASA's reference

The same method informs whether two satellites must manoeuvre; the verification discipline applies to any risk figure.

QuestionDoes our 2D probability-of-collision engine reproduce NASA CARA's values?
BaselineNASA CARA Analysis Tools test conjunctions (commit ce0f007) and Orekit's Laas2015 method.
Data53 public test conjunctions; 48 with probability at or above 1e-10 compared.
ResultPre-registered rule: every case within 1e-6 relative. 11 of 48 exceeded it, with a largest deviation of 2.9e-3: the claim was not met. S.T.A.R.'s value was compared with CARA's unadjusted Pc; against CARA's adjusted Pc, S.T.A.R. and Orekit both agree within 1e-7, Orekit within 3.3e-8.
Hardware and costSame laptop; free tools.
LimitsResearch software, not an operational collision-avoidance service. The failure is published as observed.

Orbit propagation: three programs compared

Satellite positions underpin navigation, weather and Earth-observation services.

QuestionDo independent implementations agree on where a satellite is after one orbit with the J2 effect?
BaselineSciPy integration of the same force model and hapsira (poliastro) with its own force model.
Data4 test orbits, declared inputs, tolerance 5 cm, fixed before the run.
Result4 of 4 cases within the 5 cm tolerance. The two references agree with each other within the 0.1 mm recording precision; S.T.A.R. is about 1.5 cm from both (largest 1.49 cm), the least precise of the three.
Hardware and costSame laptop; free tools; rerun with one command from the public repository.
LimitsAgreement does not prove that any of them is correct; S.T.A.R. and SciPy share the same force formulation.

Everyday and business cases

A customer register checked in one pass

An accounts office loads its table of customers and suppliers: wrong tax codes, VAT numbers and IBANs show up before an invoice is rejected.

Research it comes fromThe code-checking engines, verified row by row against python-stdnum
Workflow tests37 passed
Tests that catch injected defects99.2 % (119 of 121 mutants)

Contract templates checked in bulk

A professional or a small business downloads a non-disclosure agreement or an engagement letter already numbered, with the clauses that need a second signature already listed.

Documents8 kinds, 96 variants
Stress test3,840 PDFs, 18,952 pages, 0 failures
ComputeGoogle Cloud, 8 vCPU: 73.9 s to print them, 82.9 s to read them back
CheckEvery clause present, no excluded option, page numbering, SHA-256 seal, no overlapping text
LimitTemplates to adapt, without a lawyer's review: not legal advice

A 23-million-row table checked twice

Whoever runs large data tables wants to know whether a query gives the right answer: here the same question is answered by SQL in BigQuery and by an independent algorithm.

Data22,923,360 satellite positions (one day, one-minute steps)
Result87,847 pairs within 10 km in SQL, 87,847 with cKDTree
Differences0
Timeload 29.0 s, query 236.8 s; 917 MB billed
LimitThe same method holds for other tables only after a trial on their data

No quantum advantage is assumed anywhere on this site. Where a quantum method is studied, it is compared with the best classical method, and the classical one is the default.
