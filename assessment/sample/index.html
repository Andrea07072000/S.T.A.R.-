What you receive, shown on a real campaign: S.T.A.R.'s own orbit propagator checked against two independent integrators. A client report has the same parts, on your function.

SAMPLE — built from the public campaign XC-008 on S.T.A.R.'s own software; it is not a client deliverable.

Part 1 — Sample assessment report

Subject
star_orbit 0.2.0 — Cowell propagation with the J2 term, fixed-step RK4
Run
2026-10-03
Environment

1. The question

Does the propagator give the same satellite position as two independent implementations, within 5 cm after up to 10,000 s (167 min) of flight, on 4 orbits chosen to cover equatorial, inclined and polar cases?

2. Method

  • The same inputs are given to three implementations from different teams: S.T.A.R. Cowell fixed-step RK4 numerical integrator (star_orbit 0.2.0); poliastro/hapsira force model (J2_perturbation) and Cowell propagator (hapsira 0.18.0); SciPy Dormand-Prince 8(5,3) adaptive numerical integrator (SciPy 1.18.1).
  • Each pair of results is compared on the distance between the two computed positions (in metres).
  • The tolerance — 5 cm — was fixed before the run. A case agrees only if every pair is within it.
  • Each implementation's version is recorded; the run is repeatable with one command (section 6).

5. Verdict

4 of 4 cases agree. The largest difference is 1.49 cm, against a tolerance of 5 cm. The two reference integrators agree with each other to 0.20 mm, so the difference comes from S.T.A.R.'s side: it is the error of its fixed-step integration, small compared with the tolerance.

What this result does not say

  • It compares three computations of the same physical model (Earth's gravity with the J2 term); it does not compare them with a real satellite's tracking data.
  • Atmospheric drag, the Moon and Sun, solar pressure and the higher terms of Earth's gravity are not in the model, so they are not checked.
  • Only circular orbits and flights up to 10,000 s (167 min) were run; other orbits need their own cases.
  • Agreement is not certification; it is evidence you can rerun.

6. Rerun it yourself

Clone the public repository, install the cross-check package and run the campaign (the same commands as on the Evidence page); the evidence file below is the one this page reads, with its SHA-256 fingerprint.

git clone https://github.com/Andrea07072000/S.T.A.R.-.git
cd S.T.A.R.-/packages/star_crosscheck
python -m pip install -e .

What a client report adds

  • Your function or calculation chain, at the version you run, and your tolerance, agreed in the scope.
  • Cases chosen to reach its limits: singular geometries, extreme values, malformed input.
  • The scripts and evidence files, so your team can rerun every number.
  • A list of what was not checked and of the open questions, written down.

Without your written consent your data and code are not shared with third parties, online AI services included, and they are not used to train external models.
