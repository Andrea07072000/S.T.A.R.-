Engineering assessment

An independent check of a defined calculation, with a written scope and a report you can rerun.

01

Verify

Check a defined result against mathematical expectations, published reference values or a reference implementation.

02

Crosscheck

Run independent libraries on the same cases and record versions, input normalisation, residuals and shared dependencies.

03

Assess

Document limits, discrepancies, failure modes and reproducibility gaps, with the next steps that would close them.

What you receive

  • A comparison of your function with at least two independent implementations, on cases chosen to reach its limits: singular geometries, extreme values, malformed input.
  • The residuals, the tolerance and the versions of everything that was run.
  • The cases where it fails, refuses or silently accepts bad input.
  • The test cases and evidence files, yours to keep and rerun.
  • A plain statement of what was not tested.

→ See a sample report and a sample quote

What it is not

  • Not a certification or a qualification for flight.
  • Not a guarantee that the software has no defects.
  • Not an automated service: every assessment is scoped and carried out by a person.

Domains we can assess today

Orbit propagation and Kepler problems · Lambert and manoeuvres · orbital elements and anomalies · time scales (UTC, TAI, TT, TDB, sidereal time) · reference frames and geodesy · attitude (quaternions, MRP, Euler, Wahba) · CCSDS packets, frames and time codes · numerical methods · business calculations: spreadsheet formulas (costs, tolerances, unit conversions) and reconciliation of data exports between two systems, re-computed independently in tested code and compared with your sheet cell by cell.

Price

Quoted per scope, in writing, before any work starts. Nothing is charged for the first exchange.

How a result becomes evidence

  1. Define the scope and units
  2. Validate the inputs
  3. Record versions and provenance
  4. Run the permitted tools
  5. Compare with references
  6. Investigate discrepancies
  7. Write the evidence
  8. Report the limitations

Our commitments to you

Reply
A first technical reply within a few working hours, and always within 2 working days (48 hours) at most.
Price
The Workspace and the open-source packages are free. Tailor-made work is done only on a fixed written quote for each phase, after a free first feasibility analysis and an agreed technical scope. No hidden costs, no lock-in: you receive the source code and the tests.
Your material in the Workspace
Files are processed in your browser (Python compiled to WebAssembly) and are never sent to or stored on our servers; the only things downloaded are the page's own files from this site — its code, the Python engine and its images.
Your material in tailor-made work
Strict confidentiality, with a non-disclosure agreement before you send anything sensitive, if you wish. Your data and code are analysed in isolated environments, are never shared with third parties — online AI services included — without your written consent, and are never used to train external models. Any help from AI tools is always reviewed by an engineer and checked by deterministic tests.
