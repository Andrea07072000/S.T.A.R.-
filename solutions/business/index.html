For businesses

Purpose-built tools that perform one task reliably over time, or an independent review of a calculation your business relies on. Scope and price agreed in writing first; tests and source code delivered with the work.

Describe your requirement

Typical problems addressed

Data exports that do not reconcile

Totals that differ between systems, decimal commas read as thousands separators, missing or malformed cells. A validator detects them systematically and documents them in a report.

Critical formulas in spreadsheets

A cost, a mixing ratio or a tolerance computed in a spreadsheet that has never been re-checked. An independent recomputation shows where it agrees and where it does not.

Files that must remain unchanged

Contracts, drawings, backups: fingerprints recorded and verified automatically.

Conversions between systems

Units, coordinates, date and time formats between applications that disagree.

Example of a deliverable

The Workspace task "Check a column of numbers" is a small-scale example: it reads an Italian-format CSV, lists every conversion, flags missing and malformed cells and statistical anomalies, and returns a checked file with a verification receipt. A tailor-made tool applies the same approach to your data and your business rules.

Open the example in the Workspace

Engagement process

  1. You describe the problem with a sample of the data (no confidential material at this stage).
  2. You receive a written scope: what the tool will and will not do, how it will be verified, the price and the delivery date.
  3. No work starts and no payment is due before you accept the scope.
  4. You receive the source code, tests, installation instructions, a short manual and a statement of limits.

Defined in the written scope, before acceptance

  • Code ownership and licence
  • Duration of the included correction period after delivery
  • Where your sample data is stored and when it is deleted
  • Supported platforms and versions

Our commitments to you

Reply
A first technical reply within a few working hours, and always within 2 working days (48 hours) at most.
Price
The Workspace and the open-source packages are free. Tailor-made work is done only on a fixed written quote for each phase, after a free first feasibility analysis and an agreed technical scope. No hidden costs, no lock-in: you receive the source code and the tests.
Your material in the Workspace
Files are processed in your browser (Python compiled to WebAssembly) and are never sent to or stored on our servers; the only files downloaded are the page's own files from this site — its code, the Python engine and its images.
Your material in tailor-made work
Strict confidentiality, with a non-disclosure agreement before you send anything sensitive, if you wish. Your data and code are analysed in isolated environments, are never shared with third parties — online AI services included — without your written consent, and are never used to train external models. Any help from AI tools is always reviewed by an engineer and checked by deterministic tests.
