Open the WorkspaceWorkspace

For individuals and professionals

Verify a number before relying on it. Free tools that run in your browser, each result with a verification receipt, and open-source packages for those who write code.

Available today in the Workspace

Distance between two places

Hikers, students, surveyors, teachers: the geodesic distance and initial bearing between two points, for planning, study and verification; not for navigation or safety decisions.

Open →

Convert coordinates

Coordinates from a phone, a map or a GPS receiver: decimal degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds and UTM, verified by round-trip conversion.

Open →

Check a column of numbers

Spreadsheet exports such as meter readings, prices or measurements: missing values, formatting errors and statistical anomalies identified.

Open →

Fit a straight line

Consumption over time, calibration, growth: the trend line with its statistical uncertainty.

Open →

Check that a file is unchanged

Integrity check of a download, a backup or an attachment against the value published by its sender.

Open →

Convert units

Students, technicians, travellers: unit conversions with the conversion factor shown explicitly.

Open →

Check an Italian tax code

Codice fiscale: check character, omocodia, sex, date and place of birth.

Open →

Check an Italian VAT number

Partita IVA: check digit, office code; what it can and cannot tell.

Open →

Check an IBAN

Any country: length and check digits; for Italy also CIN, ABI, CAB and account.

Open →

Working days and deadlines

Italian holidays, Easter, your patron saint: days between two dates, or a deadline.

Open →

Check a whole table in one pass

Numbers, tax codes, VAT numbers, IBANs and dates of a table, checked together: a cleaned copy, every value read differently, one receipt.

In preparation

Further products are built on the released packages, in the order of the published catalogue. Each is released only after the same tests and reviews as every released tool; until then it is not counted.
