Clean-install test

On 19 October 2026 every public package was installed in its own fresh Linux virtual environment and its tests were run: 70 packages, 4,452 tests passed. Tested public commit: 94fcf7c. 28 tests were skipped because they need tools a clean environment does not have (the Orekit oracle, ARM and SPARC cross-compilers with the Renode emulator, AddressSanitizer, an optional weather package).

What “released” means

A package is RELEASED when its capability passes every rule the registry checks: it installs from a fixed version, its tests pass, it agrees with at least two independent implementations written by others (published tables and our own re-derivations do not count), and the latest mutation score of every module, recomputed from its result file, is at least 0.85. The registry applies these rules itself: anything that misses one stays VERIFIED. Today: 61 of the 70 public packages are RELEASED and 9 VERIFIED; the 61 released packages cover 60 capabilities (some packages share one). The registry also tracks 22 released capabilities that are not published as packages (internal audits and tools): 82 released capabilities in all. The campaign table above shows the cross-checks published so far: some test no S.T.A.R. code, some compare S.T.A.R. with one library only — its columns say which. Each package page lists the evidence recorded for that package.
