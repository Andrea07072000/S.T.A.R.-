About S.T.A.R.

What S.T.A.R. is

S.T.A.R. (Systems, Testing, Assurance & Reliability) builds independent engineering infrastructure: software, data and models for spaceflight engineering, compared with other implementations and published together with the evidence of how well they agree. Spaceflight is the first domain; the method applies to any computational engineering.

Method

  • Nothing is declared, everything is measured. A number on this site is generated from the registry, with the command that produced it.
  • Independent comparison. Every released capability is compared with at least two other libraries; shared algorithms are named.
  • Tests that are themselves tested. Mutation testing checks that the tests would notice a wrong formula (score at least 0.85).
  • Failures are published. Our own defects and their fixes are listed on the evidence page.
  • AI may help, but it is not the source of truth. AI tools help write and review; the results come from deterministic code, reference implementations and declared rules.

Who

S.T.A.R. is an independent project by Andrea Cavazzini, based in Italy. It is not, at this date, a company. Questions, collaborations and assessment requests: Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me.

Current limits

  • Prototype maturity (TRL 6 at most); no flight heritage; no certification.
  • Hardware results come from emulators (Renode), not physical boards.
  • The assessment service is carried out by a person; there is no automated online service, no account and no payment on this site.
