About S.T.A.R.

What S.T.A.R. is

S.T.A.R. is a one-person engineering project from Italy. It makes three things, in order of maturity:

  • Free tools in your browser for everyday checks — the distance between two places, coordinates, a column of numbers, a file that must not change, units — each with a receipt of every step. Open the Workspace.
  • Open-source engineering software: 70 Python packages for orbits, time, reference frames, geodesy, attitude, telemetry and numerics, each compared with programs written by others. Browse the packages.
  • Research on space safety, chips and energy, with its real status — including what failed. See the research.

Spaceflight is where the method started, because there a calculation can be checked against independent programs and published references. The same method applies to any calculation people rely on.

Method

  • Nothing is declared, everything is measured. The numbers about packages and campaigns are generated from the registry and the evidence files; research numbers come from the experiment files named next to them.
  • Independent comparison. A package is released only if it agrees with at least two implementations written by others (published tables and our own re-derivations do not count); the registry checks this itself. The evidence page shows every comparison.
  • Tests that are themselves tested. Mutation testing: thousands of small deliberate mistakes are put into the formulas, and the tests must notice at least 85 % of them — otherwise the package is not released.
  • Failures are published. Our own defects, open and fixed, are listed in the failure observatory.
  • AI may help, but it is not the source of truth. AI tools help write and review; the results come from deterministic code, reference implementations and declared rules.

Who

Andrea Cavazzini — independent researcher and developer, with studies in chemical, physical and mathematical sciences at the University of Ferrara. He designed the S.T.A.R. architecture and wrote its astrodynamics and verified-computation packages; his manuscripts are listed on ORCID 0009-0006-3166-2877 (with records on Zenodo).

S.T.A.R. is an independent research and engineering project founded and developed in Italy. It is not a company. Any professional or prototype assignment is set up contractually and fiscally, transparently and in writing, before work starts. Questions, collaborations and assessment requests: Info.and.News.S.T.A.R@proton.me — a first technical reply within a few working hours, and always within 2 working days.

Current limits

  • Prototype maturity (TRL 6 at most); no flight heritage; no certification.
  • Hardware results come from emulators (Renode, QEMU), not physical boards.
  • The assessment service is carried out by a person; there is no automated online service, no account and no payment on this site.
