Space safetyDoes an open collision-probability engine (Foster's 2D method) reproduce NASA CARA's published test values within 1e-6 (relative)?Measured — failed (pre-registered rule)48 NASA CARA test conjunctions: 37 within 1e-6 relative, 11 above (largest 2.9e-3). The pre-registered rule failed; the result is published with its numbers. Orekit agreed with the NASA values within 3.3e-8 on the same cases. Details →
Post-quantum cryptographyCan ML-KEM written only from the FIPS 203 standard reproduce the official NIST test vectors?Measured — passed240 of 240 NIST ACVP vectors reproduced byte for byte; 2,000 key exchanges interoperable with OpenSSL 4.0.1 (500 in each direction for ML-KEM-768 and ML-KEM-1024). Internal prototype, not public, not hardened, not a NIST CAVP/CMVP validation. Details →
Space linksDoes a quantum-resistant key exchange fit one CCSDS telecommand frame (TC SDLP)?ComputedNo, by arithmetic: an ML-KEM-768 ciphertext is 1,088 bytes, a TC frame carries at most 1,016 data bytes (1,024 minus header, error control and segment header). At least two frames are needed.
