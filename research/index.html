Toolchain checks on our own machine (19 and 20 October 2026)

  • 4-bit adder (minimal test circuit): Icarus Verilog 11 simulated all 512 input cases with 0 errors; Verilator 4.038 reported no warnings; Yosys 0.9 synthesised it into 20 generic cells; cocotb 2.1 checked 200 random cases.
  • CRC-32 block: Yosys formally proved its byte step equivalent, for all 2^40 inputs, to the equations derived from star_crc (a copy with one term removed was detected); cocotb simulated 658 whole messages, each CRC matching star_crc and zlib; Yosys synthesised it into 246 generic cells, 32 of them flip-flops. No foundry library was used, so no area or timing figure is claimed.
  • Stim 1.16 sampled a Bell pair 1,000 times: all 1,000 outcomes correlated (noiseless simulation, as expected).
  • All runs executed in WSL Ubuntu 22.04 on an Intel Core i5-7200U laptop. They demonstrate that the toolchain and our checks work; they are not chip-research results.
