How the parts fit together

SCL

The common language: contracts with physical units, domains and tolerances, checked before anything runs.

PHYS

The runtime that executes the work and records what ran, with which versions and inputs.

Models

Language models such as Qubit and Trinity sit in a rack. They are components, not the technical authority.

Engines

MURF and twelve other engine families: prediction, solvers, geometry, design, experiments, measurements.

Memory and evidence

Projects, results and the files that prove them, kept on your computer.

Assurance and S.T.A.R.Kontrol

Integrity of the work, permissions, local monitoring and independent checks.

The visual configuration must always match a real software configuration: a model that is not on your computer, or does not fit its memory, is not shown as running.

Six drawers organise the components. Counts are of planned families, not of finished products.

What exists today

RESEARCH PREVIEW

Trinity

Where intelligence meets verification. The largest open-weight model in the world as of 23 November 2026, to the best of our knowledge.

S.T.A.R.'s open research model for chips, space and physics: 3.56 trillion parameters, 1,152 experts in every MoE layer, 23,552 new experts ready for scientific specialisation. The Starter is a small offline package: it checks your computer before any large download, runs two S.T.A.R. verification examples (an orbit and an 8-bit adder) and explains how to reach the full weights.

The Starter's examples are computed by S.T.A.R. tools, each with its method, limits and receipt: the model proposes, the laboratory verifies.
